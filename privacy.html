Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

What the app hears

Bad Parrot uses the microphone to keep a rolling five seconds of sound in the phone's memory. Anything older than five seconds is overwritten as it goes.

What is stored

Nothing, unless you choose to share a clip. Only then is a sound file created, and it goes where you send it.

What is sent

Nothing. The app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Sound never leaves your phone unless you share it yourself.

The demo on this website

The web demo works the same way. Sound stays in the page in your browser and is never uploaded.

Other people

The app can pick up people near you. Use it with people who are in on the joke, and follow the rules on recording where you live.
